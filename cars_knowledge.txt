The Toyota Camry is known for its reliability and low maintenance costs. Common issues in older models (2007-2011) include excessive oil consumption in the 2AZ-FE engine and occasional automatic transmission hesitation. Regular oil changes every 5000 miles help prevent engine problems.

The Honda Civic is a fuel-efficient compact car popular for its longevity. Known issues include AC compressor failures in some 2016-2018 models and premature brake wear. The CVT transmission requires fluid changes every 30000 miles to stay reliable.

Fuel economy is measured in liters per 100 kilometers (L/100km) or miles per gallon (MPG). A lower L/100km means better efficiency. City driving typically consumes more fuel than highway driving due to frequent stops and acceleration.

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) includes more than the purchase price. It covers fuel, insurance, maintenance, repairs, depreciation, and registration. A cheaper car can cost more over time if it has poor fuel economy or expensive parts.

Electric vehicles (EVs) have lower running costs than gasoline cars because electricity is cheaper than fuel and they need less maintenance (no oil changes). However, they have higher upfront costs and battery replacement can be expensive after 8-10 years.

Regular maintenance extends a car's life significantly. Key tasks include oil changes, tire rotation every 10000 km, brake inspection, air filter replacement, and checking fluid levels. Skipping maintenance leads to costly repairs later.

When buying a used car, always check the vehicle history report, inspect for rust and accident damage, verify the mileage, and get a pre-purchase inspection from a mechanic. High mileage isn't always bad if the car was well maintained.

Car financing involves borrowing money to buy a car and repaying it in monthly installments with interest. The monthly payment depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term. A longer term means lower monthly payments but more total interest paid.

SUVs generally have higher fuel consumption than sedans due to their larger size and weight, but they offer more cargo space and better visibility. Compact SUVs balance efficiency and practicality for families.

The check engine light indicates a problem detected by the car's onboard diagnostics (OBD-II) system. Common causes include a loose gas cap, faulty oxygen sensor, catalytic converter issues, or spark plug problems. A diagnostic scan reveals the specific error code.

Tire maintenance is crucial for safety and fuel economy. Under-inflated tires increase fuel consumption and wear out faster. Check tire pressure monthly and rotate tires regularly to ensure even wear and longer tire life.

Depreciation is the biggest hidden cost of car ownership. Most new cars lose 20-30% of their value in the first year and about 60% over five years. Some brands like Toyota and Honda hold their value better than others.
